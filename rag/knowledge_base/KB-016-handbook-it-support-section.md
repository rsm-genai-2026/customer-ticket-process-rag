# Employee Handbook — IT Support (Excerpt from §7)

This excerpt is taken from §7 of the Helios employee handbook. The full
handbook is in BambooHR under **Documents → Handbook 2026**.

## §7.1 — When to file an IT ticket

File a ticket at `support.helios.internal` for any of the following:

- You cannot sign in to a Helios system.
- A Helios system is behaving unexpectedly (errors, missing data,
  slowness).
- You need access to a new system, dataset, or VPN profile.
- You need a new laptop, peripheral, or accessory.
- You suspect a security issue — phishing, lost device, suspicious
  email.

**Do not** file an IT ticket for:

- Personal device support (use your device manufacturer's support).
- Software not on the Helios approved list (use Workplace IT for an
  exception request first).
- Time-off questions (those go to People Ops).

## §7.2 — Response time expectations

Helios commits to the following first-response targets, measured from
ticket submission:

| Priority | First response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 (system down) | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 (degraded) | 1 hour business / 4 hours after-hours | 1 business day |
| P3 (single user impacted) | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 (cosmetic, request) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

If a ticket has not received first response within the window above,
escalate by replying to the ticket with the literal text `ESCALATE`.
This pages the support lead.

## §7.3 — What to include in a ticket

To shorten resolution time, include:

1. What you tried to do.
2. What happened (paste the exact error message if there is one).
3. What you expected.
4. Your laptop OS version (for software issues) or your location (for
   network issues).
5. A screenshot if helpful — but **never paste passwords, MFA codes,
   API keys, or other credentials** into a ticket (see KB-003).

Tickets that look like phishing-induced credential resets receive
extra scrutiny. Do not be offended if Tier-1 asks you to verify via
Slack with your manager — it is part of the standard procedure
(KB-001).

## §7.4 — Self-service

Several common needs can be self-served:

- **Password reset**: `idp.helios.internal/reset` (requires a Yubikey
  or push MFA).
- **Approved software install**: `apps.helios.internal/catalog`.
- **VPN client install**: `apps.helios.internal/catalog/meshguard`.

The self-service tools are preferred — they bypass the queue and
deliver in under 5 minutes.

## §7.5 — IT support hours

See KB-005 for the full coverage policy. The short version:

- **Business hours** (region-local): tickets of any priority are picked
  up within the SLA in §7.2.
- **After-hours**: P1 and P2 only. P3/P4 land in the overnight queue
  and are picked up at the start of the next business-day shift.

For genuine emergencies outside business hours, use the global P1
hotline at the number listed in the BambooHR contact directory.
